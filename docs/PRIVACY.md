# JPlus Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 11, 2026_

JPlus is an independent app for working with Jira Cloud. It is not affiliated
with or endorsed by Atlassian. Jira and Confluence are trademarks of Atlassian.

## The short version

JPlus doesn't collect anything about you. There is no JPlus server, no
analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app talks only to the services
you connect it to.

## What stays on your device

- **Your Jira sign-in.** The site address, email and API token you enter are
  kept in your device's Keychain. Removing the account in JPlus deletes them.
- **Your Claude API key**, if you add one, also in the Keychain.
- **Settings and recent items**, such as text size, recently opened issues and
  the last screen you were on, in the app's own storage.
- **Downloaded attachments and a search index** of issue titles, kept so
  screens load quickly. Settings › Clear deletes them.

None of this is sent to the developer.

## Who JPlus talks to

- **Your Jira Cloud site (Atlassian).** Everything you see and change in JPlus,
  such as issues, comments, versions and Confluence pages, is read from and
  written to the Atlassian site you sign in to, with your own API token.
  Atlassian handles that data under your organization's agreement with them and
  the [Atlassian Privacy Policy](https://www.atlassian.com/legal/privacy-policy).
- **Anthropic, only if you use Draft with Claude.** This optional feature is off
  until you add your own Claude API key. When you press Draft with Claude, the
  screenshot, your notes, and the project's name and issue types are sent to
  Anthropic's API to draft the ticket. JPlus asks before the first time.
  Nothing else from Jira is sent. Anthropic handles that data under its
  [commercial terms and privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Figma and other links** open in your browser only when you tap them.

## Children

JPlus is a work tool and isn't directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
